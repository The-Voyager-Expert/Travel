Which Hub to Connect Through?

Recommended routing for key destinations from anywhere on the Delta network. First hub listed = best option.
Destination RegionBest Hub(s)Notes
Europe — UK, France, NetherlandsSEAJFKATLJFK has the most frequency. ATL has LHR, CDG, AMS, FRA year-round. SEA has year-round nonstop to LHR, CDG, AMS.
Europe — Southern (Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece)JFKBOSSEAJFK has the most southern European routes. BOS new: MAD, NCE 2026. ATL: FCO, MXP, MAD, ATH. SEA: BCN, FCO summer.
Europe — Germany, Central EuropeDTWATLDTW has FRA and MUC daily year-round — fastest path to Germany from anywhere.
Europe — ScandinaviaJFKMSPCPH and ARN nonstop from JFK and MSP (seasonal). Connect via AMS (KLM) or CPH (SAS) for further Scandinavia.
Japan (Tokyo)SEALAXDTWSEA, LAX, DTW, MSP and ATL all have HND nonstop. LAX has the most frequency. SEA adds NRT nonstop from Mar 27, 2027, giving it two Tokyo options.
South Korea (Seoul Incheon)SEADTWMSPSEA, DTW, MSP, ATL and SLC all have ICN nonstop. ICN = best hub for connecting across all of East/Southeast Asia via Korean Air.
China (Shanghai)SEALAXSEA–PVG and LAX–PVG both year-round daily. DTW seasonal. Beijing: connect PVG→domestic.
Hong KongLAXLAX–HKG only, daily from Jun 2026. Every other hub is one connection via LAX.
Philippines (Manila)LAXLAX–MNL nonstop launches Mar 28, 2027 (3x weekly → daily Jun 7, A350-900). First US carrier with LAX–MNL nonstop. Until 2027 it is two connections via ICN or HND.
Australia & New ZealandLAXLAX–SYD, LAX–MEL, LAX–AKL and LAX–BNE all nonstop. Only US hub with Oceania nonstop — everywhere else is one connection via LAX.
Latin America — South AmericaATLATL has GRU, GIG, SCL, LIM, BOG, CTG, GUA, PTY all nonstop. JFK for EZE only.
AfricaATLATL is the only US Delta hub with Africa nonstop: JNB, LOS, ACC, RAK, CPT. No other hub serves Africa.
Middle East (Tel Aviv, Riyadh)JFKATLJFK–TLV resumes Sept 6, 2026 (seasonal winter). ATL–TLV suspended through Dec 18; resumes Dec 19, 2026. BOS–TLV postponed indefinitely. ATL–RUH launches Oct 23, 2026.
Portugal (LIS, OPO)JFKATLJFK–OPO daily (new 2026), JFK–LIS year-round. ATL–LIS seasonal.
Morocco (Marrakech)ATLATL–RAK daily year-round (launched Oct 2025). Then connect to Fez, Casablanca via Royal Air Maroc.
Caribbean & MexicoATLJFKMSPATL and JFK have most Caribbean nonstops. MSP best for Midwest travelers. Mexico primarily via ATL, SLC, or LAX.
