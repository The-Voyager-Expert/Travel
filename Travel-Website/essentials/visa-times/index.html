Visa Processing Times

Always verify before travel. Processing times, e-visa availability, and fee amounts change frequently — sometimes with 30 days' notice. Confirm at the official government portal for your destination and check your country's embassy page before applying. Planning tips
  • Build in buffer. "3 business days" assumes normal volume and no system issues. During summer, holiday season, or after a major policy change, processing can double. The listed times are typical minimums, not guarantees.
  • One passport photo can block everything. e-Visa rejections frequently stem from the wrong photo dimensions, poor lighting, or a recent photo that doesn't match the passport. Read the photo spec carefully before uploading.
  • Double-check your passport validity. Most countries require 6 months of validity beyond your travel dates. Some (South Africa, Brazil) require 2 blank visa pages. Check this before you apply — a short-validity passport may require renewal first.
  • Use official portals only. Third-party "visa assistance" sites often charge 2–4× the official fee for the same e-Visa. Always start from the government portal linked above or from your destination's official embassy website.
  • Save the approval email offline. Download and screenshot your e-Visa or ETA approval before departure. Some border agents require the document even when your passport has been flagged as pre-approved in their system.
  • ETIAS has been delayed past 2026. The EU's ETIAS authorization (similar to ESTA) was postponed after technical issues with the EES border system — earliest 2027. No action needed until a confirmed launch date is announced.
