Which Hub to Connect Through?

Recommended routing for key destinations from anywhere on the network. First hub listed = best option.
Destination RegionBest Hub(s)Notes
🇪🇺 Europe — UK, France, Spain, ItalyPHLDFWCLTPhiladelphia has the widest European map in the network — eighteen cities counting summer. Dallas covers the same majors from the centre of the country, Charlotte from the Southeast. JFK adds the premium A321XLR service.
🇪🇺 Europe — Central and Northern (Prague, Budapest, Copenhagen, Zurich)PHLDFWAlmost entirely Philadelphia, and almost entirely summer: Prague, Budapest, Copenhagen and Edinburgh run seasonally, Zurich year-round. Dallas adds Zurich and Amsterdam in summer.
🇪🇺 Europe — GermanyCLTDFWCharlotte–Frankfurt and Charlotte–Munich year-round; Dallas–Frankfurt year-round. American flies no other German city.
JapanDFWLAXORDDallas has both Haneda and Narita; LA has Haneda; Chicago–Narita resumes March 27, 2027. JFK–Haneda covers the East Coast.
South Korea & ChinaDFWDallas only — Seoul Incheon and Shanghai Pudong. Everything else in East and Southeast Asia is a connection on a oneworld partner from ICN, HND or LHR.
IndiaJFKJFK–Delhi is American's only India nonstop.
Australia & New ZealandLAXDFWLA–Sydney year-round; Dallas adds Brisbane and Auckland in season, and LA adds Auckland. Dallas–Sydney is not flown.
Middle East (Tel Aviv)JFKJFK–Tel Aviv resumes January 6, 2027. No other American hub flies the Middle East.
Africa—American flies no African destination from any hub. Use a oneworld partner via LHR, MAD or DOH.
South America — Brazil, Argentina, Chile, PeruMIADFWJFKMiami is the fortress: São Paulo, Rio, Buenos Aires, Santiago, Lima, Montevideo, plus Quito, Guayaquil and four Colombian cities. Dallas and JFK add São Paulo and Buenos Aires.
Colombia, Ecuador, GuyanaMIAMiami flies Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Cartagena, Barranquilla, Quito, Guayaquil and Georgetown. Dallas has Bogotá; JFK has Georgetown.
Central AmericaMIADFWMiami has every capital plus Roatán and Liberia. Dallas covers the same countries. Charlotte and Chicago add Belize City, Guatemala City and Liberia in season.
🇲🇽 MexicoDFWMIAPHXDallas has thirteen Mexican cities including Morelia, Oaxaca, Querétaro and León — the deepest non-resort map any US airline flies. Miami adds Mérida and Tulum.
CubaMIAMiami is the only US hub with Cuba: Havana, Holguín, Camagüey, Santiago de Cuba and Varadero.
CaribbeanMIACLTPHLMiami serves thirty-one Caribbean airports and Charlotte eighteen — between them, effectively every island with a runway. Philadelphia and JFK cover the majors.
🇨🇦 CanadaDFWCLTMIAToronto and Vancouver from most hubs; Montréal from Dallas and Miami; Calgary from Dallas year-round and elsewhere in summer; Halifax from Philadelphia in summer.
