Which Hub to Connect Through?

Recommended routing for key destinations from anywhere on the network. First hub listed = best option.
Destination RegionBest Hub(s)Notes
🇪🇺 Europe — UK, France, Netherlands, GermanyEWRIADORDNewark alone flies 16 European cities year-round. Dulles is the quieter twin with 13. Chicago covers the same ground from the Midwest, and Frankfurt/Munich are reachable nonstop from Denver, Houston and San Francisco too.
🇪🇺 Europe — Mediterranean and the islandsEWRIADAlmost entirely a summer network, and almost entirely Newark: Bari, Palermo, Split, Dubrovnik, Málaga, Palma, Faro, Funchal, Ponta Delgada, Santiago de Compostela. Dulles adds Athens, Nice and Venice.
🇪🇺 Europe — Ireland, Scotland, Iceland, GreenlandEWRORDIADDublin and Edinburgh year-round from Newark and Dulles. Shannon, Glasgow, Reykjavík and Nuuk are summer only — Nuuk is the first scheduled US–Greenland service.
JapanSFOLAXEWRHaneda from San Francisco, LA, Newark, Dulles and Chicago; Narita from all of those plus Denver, Houston and Guam. San Francisco adds Osaka and, from December 2026, Sapporo.
South Korea, China, TaiwanSFOLAXSan Francisco has Seoul, Shanghai, Beijing and Taipei. LA has Shanghai, Beijing and Hong Kong. Newark adds Seoul from September 4, 2026.
Southeast Asia (Singapore, Bangkok, Manila, Ho Chi Minh City)SFOLAXSFO–SIN is one of the longest flights in the world and the only US nonstop to Singapore on United. Bangkok and Ho Chi Minh City are LA only; Manila runs from both SFO and Guam.
IndiaEWRNewark–Delhi is United's only India nonstop.
Australia & New ZealandSFOLAXIAHSFO has Sydney, Brisbane, Auckland and — in season — Adelaide and Christchurch. LA has Sydney. Houston adds a seasonal Sydney, the longest Oceania route from the US interior.
AfricaIADEWRDulles is the gateway: Accra, Lagos and Cape Town. Newark adds Cape Town and Johannesburg, and Marrakesh in summer. No other United hub flies Africa.
Middle East (Tel Aviv, Dubai)ORDEWRChicago–Tel Aviv is year-round. Newark–Tel Aviv resumes September 8, 2026; Dulles–Tel Aviv is suspended. Newark–Dubai is suspended.
South America — Brazil, Argentina, Chile, PeruIAHEWRHouston is the gateway: São Paulo, Rio, Buenos Aires, Santiago (seasonal), Lima, Quito, Medellín, Bogotá, Georgetown. Newark adds São Paulo, Lima and Bogotá; Dulles and Chicago add São Paulo.
Central AmericaIAHEWRHouston flies all of it — Belize City, Guatemala City, San Salvador, Tegucigalpa, San Pedro Sula, Managua, Roatán, Liberia. Newark covers the same countries with fewer frequencies.
🇲🇽 MexicoIAHEWRORDHouston has eleven Mexican cities, well beyond the resorts — Querétaro, León, Morelia-adjacent Bajío, Veracruz, Mérida, Monterrey, Guadalajara. Every other hub flies the beach map.
CaribbeanEWRIAHORDNewark is the deepest: 23 islands counting the summer-only routes. Houston and Chicago cover the majors.
🇨🇦 CanadaORDDENIAHToronto and Vancouver from most hubs; Calgary and Edmonton from Denver, Houston and Chicago; Montréal and Ottawa summer only.
