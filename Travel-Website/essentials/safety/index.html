Over 230 cities and islands with a guide on this site, placed in the four US State Department advisory levels — normal precautions, increased caution, reconsider travel, do not travel — with the current standing notices called out above the list. Advisory levels move with events and can be raised or lowered without warning; check the live advisory on travel.state.gov before you book or fly.

UAE, Qatar, Oman & Jordan — Level 3 as of 2026. The State Dept issued Level 3 "Reconsider Travel" advisories for the UAE, Qatar, Oman, and Jordan citing regional armed-conflict risks — including the threat of drone and missile attacks following US–Iran hostilities — and authorized non-emergency US government personnel to depart the UAE and Qatar. Monitor the advisories for the UAE, Qatar, Oman, and Jordan closely before any travel to Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Doha, Muscat, or Petra.
