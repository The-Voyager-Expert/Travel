A living-history complex of four fully restored homes spanning 1709 to 1850 — the Schreuderhuis, Blettermanhuis, Grosvenor House, and OM Bergh House — each furnished to its period with guides in historical costume showing how settler life evolved across the colony's first two centuries.

3.Huguenot Memorial Museum · Franschhoek
A museum and monument complex honouring the 200 French Huguenot refugees who settled the Franschhoek valley from 1688, bringing viticulture and names — de Villiers, du Toit, du Plessis — that still define the region; the three-arched 1948 monument stands in a garden directly behind the museum entrance.

🍮 Local Tastes
Bobotie
South Africa's national dish: spiced minced meat, sweetened with apricot jam and curry spices, baked under a golden egg custard. A Cape Malay recipe brought by 17th-century enslaved cooks from the Indonesian archipelago.
📍 Bo-Kaap Kombuis · Biesmiellah
Cape Malay Koeksisters
Braided dough deep-fried and dunked in spiced syrup, then rolled in desiccated coconut — the Cape Malay cousin of the plainer Afrikaner koeksister, sold from Bo-Kaap doorsteps especially around Eid.
📍 Biesmiellah · Rose's Corner Cafe

🌊 Two Oceans, One Afternoon

The Cape Peninsula is popularly sold as the place "where two oceans meet" — but the Atlantic and Indian Ocean actually mix much farther east, off Cape Agulhas. What Cape Point really offers is something quieter and just as strange: standing on one cliff, the cold Benguela current runs up the Atlantic side, and on the other, the warmer Agulhas current runs up the False Bay side — close enough to feel the temperature shift in the wind on your face within a few hundred metres.

Robben Island's most striking room is not Nelson Mandela's cell but the lime quarry where he and other prisoners worked, breaking rock in glare so intense it damaged many prisoners' eyesight permanently. A single small cairn of stones now marks the spot — placed there in 1995 by former prisoners returning for a reunion, each dropping one stone, an unplanned memorial that the museum has left exactly as it was made.
