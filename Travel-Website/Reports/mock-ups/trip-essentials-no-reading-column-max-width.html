Trip Essentials
Entry Requirements

Visa requirements vary significantly depending on your passport and destination. Most countries offer visa-on-arrival or e-visa options for tourism, but the process and eligibility differ. It's important to check requirements well in advance of travel — some countries require applications six weeks or more before your arrival date.

A valid passport with at least six months of remaining validity beyond your intended stay is required by the majority of countries worldwide. Some destinations also require a minimum number of blank pages. Check with your destination's official embassy website or the travel advisory service of your home country.
