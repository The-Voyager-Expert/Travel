Recommended routing for key destinations from SEA or anywhere on the Delta network. First hub listed = best option.

Europe — Southern (Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece)JFKBOSSEAJFK has most southern European routes. BOS new: MAD, NCE 2026. SEA: BCN, FCO summer. ATL: FCO, MXP, MAD, ATH.
Europe — Germany, Central EuropeDTWATLDTW has FRA and MUC daily year-round — fastest path to Germany from anywhere.
Europe — ScandinaviaJFKMSPCPH and ARN nonstop from JFK and MSP (seasonal). Connect via AMS (KLM) or CPH (SAS) for further Scandinavia.
Japan (Tokyo Haneda)SEALAXDTWSEA, LAX, DTW, MSP, ATL all have HND nonstop. SEA = best timing from Pacific NW. LAX = most frequency.
South Korea (Seoul Incheon)SEADTWMSPSEA, DTW, MSP, ATL, SLC all have ICN nonstop. ICN = best hub for connecting across all of East/Southeast Asia via Korean Air.
China (Shanghai)SEALAXSEA–PVG and LAX–PVG both year-round daily. DTW seasonal. Beijing: connect PVG→domestic.
Hong KongLAXLAX–HKG only, daily from Jun 2026. From SEA: SEA→LAX→HKG = 2 flights, fastest routing.
Philippines (Manila)LAXLAX–MNL nonstop launches Mar 28, 2027 (3x weekly → daily Jun 7, A350-900). First US carrier with LAX–MNL nonstop. From SEA: 1 connection via LAX. Until 2027: 2 connections via ICN or HND.
Australia & New ZealandLAXLAX–SYD, LAX–MEL, LAX–AKL, LAX–BNE all nonstop. From SEA = 1 connection. Only US hub with Oceania nonstop.
Latin America — South AmericaATLATL has GRU, GIG, SCL, LIM, BOG, CTG, GUA, PTY all nonstop. JFK for EZE only.
AfricaATLATL is the only US Delta hub with Africa nonstop: JNB, LOS, ACC, RAK, CPT. No other hub serves Africa.
Middle East (Tel Aviv, Riyadh)JFKATLJFK–TLV resumes Sept 6, 2026 (seasonal winter). ATL–TLV suspended through Dec 18; resumes Dec 19, 2026. BOS–TLV postponed indefinitely. ATL–RUH launches Oct 23, 2026.
Portugal (LIS, OPO)JFKATLJFK–OPO daily (new 2026), JFK–LIS year-round. ATL–LIS seasonal. Best 1-connection from SEA.
Morocco (Marrakech)ATLATL–RAK daily year-round (launched Oct 2025). Then connect to Fez, Casablanca via Royal Air Maroc.
Caribbean & MexicoATLJFKMSPATL and JFK have most Caribbean nonstops. MSP best for Midwest travelers. Mexico primarily via ATL, SLC, or LAX.
