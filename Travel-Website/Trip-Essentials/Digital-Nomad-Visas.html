Portugal — D8 Digital Nomad Visa
Residency visa; pathway to long-term residence
Europe
Income minimum
~€3,040 / mo
Visa fee
~€90
Processing
60–90 days
Initial stay
1–2 years (renewable)
Key notes: Apply at the Portuguese consulate in your home country. You must show proof of employment or freelance income for the past 3 months. Health insurance with €30,000 coverage required. After 5 years, eligible for permanent residency or citizenship.

Japan — Digital Nomad Visa
Status of Residence: Designated Activities · launched April 2024
Asia-Pacific
Income minimum
~¥10M / yr (~$68k USD)
Visa fee
~¥3,000 (~$20 USD)
Processing
Varies by consulate
Initial stay
Up to 6 months (not renewable)
Key notes: Available to US citizens (Japan has a tax treaty with the US). Must show proof of remote work, health insurance covering ¥10M (~$68k), and income from outside Japan. Non-renewable — must leave Japan for 6 months before reapplying. Apply at a Japanese consulate in the US before departure.
Japan MOFA
