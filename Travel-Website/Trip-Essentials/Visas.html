🇹🇭 Thailand30 days Bangkok · Chiang Mai · Phuket File the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) online within 72 hours before arrival. Effective July 2026, the visa-free exemption is 30 days (reduced from 60; Royal Gazette published). Verify current rules at tdac.immigration.go.th before travel.
Stay cut: Thailand's visa-free stay was reduced from 60 to 30 days effective July 2026 (Royal Gazette published). Plan accordingly — overstays incur fines.

A paid electronic permit (ETA) you must get approved before boarding. Not a visa — usually approved fast online or via an app.
🇬🇧 United KingdomUK ETA Cambridge · Edinburgh · Glasgow · London · Oxford Required since Feb 25, 2026 for US citizens. £20, approved in minutes via the UK ETA app, valid 2 years, stays up to 6 months. Also covers Northern Ireland.
🇦🇺 AustraliaETA (601) Melbourne · Sydney ETA subclass 601 via the Australian ETA app. AUD 20, multiple entries for 1 year, up to 90 days per visit.
🇳🇿 New ZealandNZeTA Queenstown · Wellington NZeTA (NZD 17 app / 23 web) plus the mandatory IVL tourism levy (NZD 100 since Oct 2024), paid together. Valid 2 years, up to 90 days per visit.
eVisa — apply online before travel eVisa 5 destinations
A paid electronic visa applied for online before travel. Approved digitally — no embassy visit needed.
🇧🇷 BrazileVisa Aracaju · Curitiba · Florianópolis · Fortaleza · Foz do Iguaçu · João Pessoa · Maceió · Natal · Olinda · Porto Alegre · Recife · Rio de Janeiro · Salvador · São Luís · São Paulo Visa reinstated Apr 10, 2025; permanent eVisa from Jan 1, 2026. Apply at brazil.vfsevisa.com — about US$80.90, usually issued within about 10 business days (apply well ahead), multiple-entry valid up to 10 years, 90 days per visit. Airlines check it at boarding.
🇹🇷 TurkeyeVisa Istanbul Apply at evisa.gov.tr before travel — US$50, multiple-entry, valid 180 days from issue, up to 90 days per visit. Processed quickly online; airlines verify at check-in.
🇱🇦 LaoseVisa Luang Prabang Apply at evisa.laopdr.gov.la — 30-day single-entry tourist eVisa, ~USD 50, typically approved within 3 business days. Visa on arrival (~USD 35) also available at Luang Prabang International Airport.
🇻🇳 VietnameVisa Hanoi · Hoi An Apply at evisa.gov.vn — 90-day single-entry ~US$25 or multiple-entry ~US$50, processed in 3–5 business days. Visa on arrival also available at international airports with a pre-approved letter. US citizens are not covered by Vietnam's 45-day visa exemption and need this eVisa for mainland travel. The one carve-out: Phú Quốc island allows a 30-day visa-free stay if you arrive there directly from abroad. Vietnam has 83 international entry points accepting eVisa holders.
🇪🇬 EgypteVisa · $30 Cairo Apply online at visa2egypt.gov.eg — US$30, approved in 24–72 hours; apply at least 5 days before travel. Visa on arrival (also US$30 since March 2026) is available at Cairo International Airport, but the pre-arranged eVisa is faster.
Visa on arrival — issued at the airport Visa on arrival 3 destinations
Visa issued at the airport on arrival — no advance application needed.
🇮🇩 IndonesiaVisa on arrival Bali Visa on arrival at major airports (Ngurah Rai/Bali, etc.) — IDR 500,000 (~US$33), 30 days, extendable once for 30 more days. Cash or card at the airport counter before immigration, or apply for the e-VoA online beforehand. Bali arrivals also pay a separate Bali Tourist Levy (IDR 150,000, ~US$10).
🇯🇴 Jordan40 JOD (~$56) Petra US citizens receive a single-entry visa on arrival at Queen Alia International Airport (AMM) and most border crossings. Valid for 30 days. Consider the Jordan Pass — if you plan to visit Petra, the pass covers the visa fee and Petra entry ticket when purchased before arrival and staying a minimum of 3 nights.
🇳🇵 Nepal15 / 30 / 90 days Pokhara US citizens can get a Tourist Visa on Arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) or land borders — USD 30 (15 days), USD 50 (30 days), or USD 125 (90 days). Apply online in advance at online.nepalimmigration.gov.np to save time at arrival.
Visa required — arrange before travel Visa required 2 destinations
A traditional visa arranged in advance — apply through the embassy or official portal before you travel.
🇧🇹 BhutanVisa required Paro All visitors (except Indian, Bangladeshi, and Maldivian nationals) must obtain a Bhutan Visa through the Department of Immigration at doi.gov.bt. A Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) of USD 100/day applies. A 5% GST on tourism services (hotels, guides, transport — excl. SDF) took effect January 1, 2026. Visa is processed online before arrival.
🇨🇳 ChinaVisa needed Beijing · Chongqing · Shanghai · Zhangjiajie US citizens need a tourist visa arranged in advance. (A 240-hour / 10-day visa-free transit exists only if you're continuing to a third country and entering/exiting via eligible ports — not for a round-trip China visit.)

Europe Digital Nomad Visas 5 programs
🇵🇹 Portugal1 yr, renewable D8 Digital Nomad Visa · Min income ~€3,040/mo · Fee ~€90 · Processing 60–90 days · Apply via Portuguese consulate or VFS Global · Opens path to permanent residence after 5 years. NHR tax regime may apply — confirm at application time.
🇪🇸 Spain1 yr, renewable to 5 Digital Nomad Visa (Startup Law) · Min income ~€2,646/mo · Fee ~€74 · Processing ~20 business days in-country; longer at consulates · ≥80% of income must come from non-Spanish sources. Beckham Law tax benefits available.
🇭🇷 Croatia1 yr (non-renewable) Digital Nomad Residence Permit · Min income ~$2,300/mo · Fee ~€55 · Processing ~1 month · Apply at mup.gov.hr · No Croatian tax liability. Cannot extend — must leave and reapply after a gap.
🇪🇪 EstoniaUp to 1 yr Digital Nomad Visa (D-visa) · Min income €3,500/mo gross · Fee ~€80–100 · Processing 15–30 days · Apply via Police and Border Guard Board · High income threshold. e-Residency is a separate program and does not grant right of residence.
🇩🇪 Germany1–3 yrs, renewable Freelance Visa (Freiberufler Visum) · No fixed income minimum — must demonstrate financial self-sufficiency · Fee ~€75 · Processing 6–12 weeks · Apply at German consulate · Typically for IT, creative, and consulting professions. Requires showing qualified clients or economic contribution to Germany.
Americas Digital Nomad Visas 3 programs
🇲🇽 Mexico1 yr, renewable to 4 Temporary Resident Visa (Residente Temporal) · Min income ~$1,620/mo OR ~$27,000 in savings · Fee ~$36 · Processing ~10 days at Mexican consulate · Most popular Latin American option. No Mexican taxes on foreign-sourced income. After 4 years, eligible to apply for permanent residence.
🇨🇴 Colombia1–2 yrs, renewable Digital Nomad / Migrant Visa · Min income ~3× Colombian minimum wage (~$700–1,000/mo) · Fee $54 · Processing 3–5 business days · Apply at cancilleria.gov.co · One of the most accessible programs globally. Bogotá and Medellín are top nomad hubs.
🇧🇧 Barbados12 months Welcome Stamp · Must demonstrate financial self-sufficiency · Fee $1,500 (individual) / $2,000 (with dependents) · Apply at visitbarbados.org · No local income tax on foreign earnings. Program can be used annually.
Asia-Pacific Remote work options 3 programs
🇬🇪 GeorgiaUp to 365 days No visa needed — US citizens enter visa-free for up to a year per visit (see Entry Requirements tab). No formal digital nomad visa program required. Tbilisi is one of the most established nomad hubs: low cost of living, fast internet, no Georgian income tax on foreign-sourced income for short stays.
🇮🇩 IndonesiaUp to ~6 months B211A Social-Cultural Visa (most common nomad path) · No set income minimum · Fee ~$150–200 · 60 days, extendable via 5×30-day extensions · Apply at imigrasi.go.id · Widely used for Bali. A formal digital nomad visa category was under review in 2025 — check current status before applying.
🇹🇭 Thailand10 years (LTR) Long-Term Resident Visa — Work From Thailand Professional · Min income $80,000/yr for 2+ consecutive years · Fee $600 · Processing ~20 business days · Apply at ltrthailand.boi.go.th · High income threshold. Most remote workers instead use the SMART visa, Thailand Elite, or the standard tourist stay with an extension.
Verify before applying: income thresholds, fees, and processing times change frequently. Always check the official embassy or immigration website before submitting an application. Tax treatment for nomad visa holders varies by country and individual situation — consult a local tax advisor.
