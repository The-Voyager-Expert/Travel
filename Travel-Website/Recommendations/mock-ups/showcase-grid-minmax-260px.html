Finding: showcase-grid-minmax-260px · 2026-08-06 · Target: web-travel-style.css lines 531, 705

At viewport widths 601–640px (just above the 600px mobile breakpoint), effective grid width is ~570–610px. With minmax(300px, 1fr), two 300px columns need 600px — so at 601px viewport only one column fits. This creates a jarring single-column layout on small desktops and large tablets. Lowering to 260px gives two columns from ~540px effective width.
